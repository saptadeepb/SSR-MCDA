NOTE ON UNITS: the WebFetch summarizing model sometimes rescales OBS_VALUE to
"millions USD" with 2-decimal rounding instead of returning the raw
USD-thousand figure. Where this happened (calls 9-10 below), values were
back-converted (*1000) to USD thousand for CSV consistency with the unit
column; this introduces a precision loss of up to ~$1,000 (i.e. sub-0.001%
relative error) versus the true OBS_VALUE, since the model already rounded
before we saw it. This is a rounding artifact of the fetch, not an invented
number -- flagged as a data-quality caveat in the coverage report. Later
calls' prompts were NOT changed (same standard prompt); Metals-type calls
happened to come back already in thousand-scale (matching prior verified
usa-chn figure exactly), so no conversion was applied there.
